What GLP-1 actually does to your body

GLP-1 receptor agonists work with your biology — not against it — to produce meaningful, lasting results.

🧠

Reduces Hunger Signals

GLP-1 acts on the hypothalamus to reduce appetite and cravings, helping you feel satisfied with less food.

🔥

Boosts Metabolism

Regulates blood sugar and insulin response so your body burns fat more efficiently throughout the day.

❤️

Improves Heart Health

Clinical trials show GLP-1 agonists significantly reduce cardiovascular risk, blood pressure, and inflammation.

💉

Regulates Blood Sugar

Originally for type-2 diabetes, GLP-1 medications stabilize glucose levels and reduce insulin resistance.

⚡

Slows Digestion

Slows gastric emptying so nutrients absorb gradually — keeping you fuller longer and preventing spikes.

Your questions, answered.

How does Tromso work?+
Tromso connects you with licensed medical providers online. Complete a brief intake, meet with a provider via telehealth, and if eligible, receive a prescription shipped from a licensed US pharmacy directly to your door.
How do I know this is safe?+
All medications are prescribed by licensed clinicians and dispensed by licensed US pharmacies. Your provider reviews your full health history before prescribing.
How does the sign-up process work?+
Complete a quick online intake, a provider reviews your case, and if approved, your prescription ships within 24–48 hours. Most patients receive their first order in 2–3 business days.
What states/jurisdictions are eligible?+
Tromso currently serves most US states. Eligibility is confirmed during the intake process. Some states may have limitations on specific medications.
How does the pricing work?+
You pay a flat monthly fee for your medication — no membership, no hidden charges. Plans start at $149/month and include telehealth visits, provider messaging, and free shipping.
What if I need to cancel?+
You can cancel anytime with no fees or penalties. Simply contact our support team before your next billing date.
Is the medication GLP-1 / Semaglutide / GIP-1?+
Yes. Our compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide contain the same active pharmaceutical ingredients as branded medications, prepared by licensed US compounding pharmacies.
Do you offer non-injectable options?+
Yes — we offer oral semaglutide tablets. Your provider will help determine the best option for your goals and health history.
What is the drug I'll be taking?+
Depending on your prescription, you may receive compounded semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, sermorelin, or others. Your provider will explain your specific prescription in detail.
Where do my prescriptions come from?+
Prescriptions are fulfilled by licensed US compounding pharmacies that meet strict sterile compounding standards.
How do I take my medication?+
Injection medications are self-administered weekly or as directed. Oral medications are taken daily. Your provider will walk you through the process with clear instructions.
How is it shipped?+
Medications ship free via 2-day shipping in discreet packaging. Temperature-sensitive medications include cold-chain packaging.
How much weight will I lose?+
Clinical studies show patients lose an average of 15–22% of body weight over 68 weeks. Results vary based on starting weight, adherence, diet, and exercise habits.
How do I contact support?+
Reach us at hello@tromsomedia.com or call 1 343 303 5101. Our team is available 24/7 and typically responds within 1–2 hours.
